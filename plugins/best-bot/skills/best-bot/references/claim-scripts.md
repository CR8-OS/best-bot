# Claim scripts

Give the user the script. The user makes the request. Best Bot never opens
chat, never dials, never submits a form.

Fill every bracket with real values before handing it over. A script with an
unfilled placeholder in it is an embarrassment waiting to happen on a customer
service call.

## Case 1: Best Buy lowered its own price

Usually the strongest claim. Lead with the policy language.

> Hi, I'd like a price match on a recent purchase under your Price Match
> Guarantee. Order number [ORDER NUMBER], placed [ORDER DATE] and delivered
> [DELIVERY DATE]. It's the [ITEM NAME], SKU [SKU], and I paid [PRICE PAID]
> before tax. The price on BestBuy.com for that same SKU is now [CURRENT
> PRICE], sold by Best Buy. That's within my return and exchange period.
> [I'm a My Best Buy Plus/Total member, so my window runs about 60 days / my
> window is 15 days from delivery]. Your policy says if you lower your own
> price during that period you'll match it on request. Could you refund the
> difference, [DIFFERENCE] before tax, plus the tax on it, to my original
> payment method?

## Case 2: A qualified competitor is cheaper

> Hi, I'd like to request a price match under your Price Match Guarantee.
> Order number [ORDER NUMBER], placed [ORDER DATE], for the [ITEM NAME], SKU
> [SKU]. I paid [PRICE PAID] before tax. [COMPETITOR] currently has the same
> model, [MODEL NUMBER], new and in stock, sold and shipped by [COMPETITOR]
> itself, for [COMPETITOR PRICE]. It's the identical configuration, [KEY
> SPECS]. I'm still within my return and exchange period. Could you match that
> price and refund the difference, [DIFFERENCE] before tax, plus the tax?

Have the URL ready to paste if asked.

## If they push back

- **"That's a limited-time sale."** Ask which exclusion applies. "Special
  daily or hourly sales" is a real, named exclusion in the published policy.
  If that is what they invoke, do not press it. If they are describing an
  ordinary price reduction rather than a daily or hourly deal, the "we lower
  our own price during the return and exchange period" clause covers it, and
  a polite request for supervisor review is reasonable.
- **"That seller is a marketplace seller."** If the find really is
  third-party, the agent is right. Confirm before pressing, Best Bot should
  have caught this before notifying.
- **"You have to come to a store."** BestBuy.com purchases are handled by
  chat or phone. Store visits are for in-store purchases.
- **Flat refusal, still early in the window.** Returning and rebuying at the
  lower price reaches the same result, but check the restocking fee first.
  Activatable devices carry $45; drones, cameras, lenses, camcorders,
  projectors and several other categories carry 15%. Do not raise this
  fallback at all unless the drop clearly exceeds the fee, and state the fee
  when raising it.

## Channel note

Phone (1-888-237-8289) is the reliable channel. Online chat exists but has
limited availability. Neither request can be completed in a store or by email
for a BestBuy.com purchase.

## Tone note

The agent on the other end did not set the price and has a script of their
own. Polite and specific gets further than aggrieved. Keep the user's message
short, factual, and easy to act on.
