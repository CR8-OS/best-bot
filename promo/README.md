# Promo

Launch and social material for Best Bot. Screenshots, memes, cards, anything
made to post rather than to ship.

**Contents of this folder are deliberately not committed.** The `.gitignore`
excludes everything here except this README.

The reason is licensing. The repo is MIT, which grants anyone the right to use,
modify, sublicense and sell what is in it. That is a fine promise to make about
original work. It is not a promise anybody can make about a meme template, a
show still, a stock photo, or a screenshot of someone else's product. Posting
that material is ordinary. Republishing it under a license you do not hold is a
different thing, and the folder boundary is what keeps the two apart.

Original promo assets that *are* safe to license (the logo, the social card)
live in `plugins/best-bot/assets/` and are committed normally.
